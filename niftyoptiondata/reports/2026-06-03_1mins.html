🕐 RESOLUTION KEYS

5-min takes the max CE spike and min PE drop within each 5-min window — good for cutting noise.

📋 VIEW KEYS

E Show Events sidebar S Spikes only X Drops only C CE side only A PE side only O Opposite confirmations B Bullish confirms (CE↑ PE↓) R Bearish confirms (PE↑ CE↓)

📖 READING SIGNALS

▲ CE spike — calls gaining, bullish pressure
▼ CE drop — calls unwinding, bearish pressure
▲ PE spike — puts gaining, bearish pressure
▼ PE drop — puts unwinding, bullish pressure

CE↑ + PE↓ same strike = BULLISH confirm
PE↑ + CE↓ same strike = BEARISH confirm

⚙ SPIKE THRESHOLDS

Bars highlighted bright when they cross these thresholds.

🚀 FAST WORKFLOW

1. Press O → check opposite confirms

4. Press V → confirm with volume
5. Toggle 5 → 5-min to see macro trend
6. Toggle 1 → back to 1-min for entry timing
